Appliance Energy Consumption in Australia

In Australia, residential appliance energy consumption accounts for a significant portion of household utility bills and carbon footprints. As energy efficiency standards evolve under the Nationwide House Energy Rating Scheme (NatHERS) and the Equipment Energy Efficiency (E3) program, understanding appliance star ratings is key to making informed choices.
